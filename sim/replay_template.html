Green light or green band on the avenue. Grey squares are queued vehicles, blue squares are vehicles driving to the next intersection (30 s).

Time-space diagram: each row is one intersection. A green bar shows when its avenue light is green; each thin line is a group of vehicles leaving one intersection and arriving at the next. When the lines meet the next green bar, there is a green wave.

Distance to the green wave is how far the offsets are from the ideal ones (0 s = perfect). Seed 17, normal or peak demand, 90 s cycle.
